## Nowbar Meter 1.4.0

- Complete German interface, including settings, notifications and update dialogs.
- Independent package name; no Kakao T identity in the standard app.
- Updates and downloads now use this maintained repository.

**Installation:** Uninstall the earlier debug test build before installing this release and record your settings first. The original realMoai app can remain installed; stop its monitor to avoid duplicate displays. Now Bar availability depends on your Samsung firmware.
